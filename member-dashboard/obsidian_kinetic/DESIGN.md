## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-intensity performance, modern biometric precision, and dynamic athletic motivation. Tailored for an elite, connected gym community, the aesthetic fuses sleek dark-mode immersion with frosted glassmorphism and radiant energetic highlights.

### Aesthetics & Tone
- **Atmospheric & Immersive:** Deep obsidian and carbon base layers eliminate screen glare in low-lit gym settings, creating a focused, high-contrast visual stage.
- **Translucent Layering:** Frosted glass panels (`backdrop-blur`) establish tactile depth and floating elevation without visual bulk.
- **Electric Energy:** High-luminance orange and ember accents trigger action, urgency, and motivation, contrasted against athletic mint green for verified milestones and check-ins.
- **Biometric Precision:** Clean visual metrics, dynamic data readouts, and crisp telemetry tokens evoke high-performance sports analytics.

## Colors

The palette relies on deep carbon environments paired with luminous accent channels to maintain clarity, hierarchy, and athletic vigor across all lighting conditions.

### Primary & Energetic Accents
- **Electric Gym Orange (`#FF5E1E`):** Primary driver for high-priority CTAs, active exercise sets, and key interactive focal points.
- **Neon Ember (`#FF7A00` / `#FF3D00`):** Secondary warm gradations, streak indicators, and dynamic telemetry progress fills.

### Functional & Success
- **Athletic Mint Green (`#10B981`):** Active status badges, completed sets, verified NFC check-ins, and positive heart-rate recovery bands.
- **Warning & Hazard (`#F59E0B`):** Rest timer countdowns and capacity thresholds.
- **Error / Critical (`#EF4444`):** Hardware sync drops and missed intervals.

### Dark Obsidian Substrates
- **Base Canvas (`#0B0F17`):** Primary canvas ground. Pure deep obsidian tone.
- **Layer 1 Surface (`#111827`):** Recessed telemetry wells, underlying sheets, and structural cards.
- **Frosted Translucent (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.7)` / `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`): Floating glass containers paired with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` edge highlights.
- **Text Primary (`#F9FAFB`):** High-contrast display headlines and critical readouts.
- **Text Secondary (`#9CA3AF`):** Telemetry units, sub-labels, and metadata.
- **Text Disabled / Muted (`#4B5563`):** Inactive structural dividers and dormant parameters.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for commanding, high-impact titles, numerical workout dashboards, and UI labels, with **Inter** for sustained readability in routine body text, descriptions, and exercise logs.

### Application Rules
- **Numerical Telemetry:** All real-time heart rate stats, rep counts, and calorie burn metrics employ `telemetry-num` or uppercase bold variants of Plus Jakarta Sans to maximize glanceability mid-workout.
- **Case Formatting:** Use uppercase styling with widened tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.04em` to `0.06em`) for `label-md` and `label-sm` indicators (e.g., `ZONE 4`, `SET 3/5`, `ACTIVE PASS`).
- **Responsive Sizing:** Use the `-mobile` scale overrides on smaller handheld displays to keep workout split tables and biometric cards within single viewport scrolls.

## Layout & Spacing

This mobile-first design utilizes a flexible fluid grid engineered for rapid one-handed navigation, dynamic workout routines, and high-density telemetry displays.

### Grid & Viewport Model
- **Handheld Devices (< 640px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` outer canvas margin (`margin`) and `0.75rem` intra-column gutter (`gutter-sm`). Essential workout actions align within the bottom 40% thumb zone.
- **Tablet / Equipment Kiosk (640px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins (`margin-lg`) and `1rem` gutters (`gutter`). Supports side-by-side split screens for workout telemetry alongside routine exercise steps.
- **Rhythm Base:** Strict 4px base increments (`space-xs` through `space-2xl`) to ensure tight horizontal metric badges and structured stat rows.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent glassmorphism layers, micro-lit borders, and radiant ember glows, avoiding muddy or opaque drop shadows.

### Glassmorphism & Tonal Surfaces
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Solid background tone `#0B0F17`.
- **Level 1 (Docked Modules & Recessed Trays):** Background `#111827` at 100% opacity or subtle inset drop `inset 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`.
- **Level 2 (Glass Containers & Cards):** Background `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.65)` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a crisp perimeter border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Overlays & Modals):** Background `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, perimeter border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, and a subtle dark floor shadow `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.

### Chromatic Light Flares
- **Active State Glow:** Active interactive controls or gym check-in passes emit a soft localized glow using `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(255, 94, 30, 0.35)`.
- **Mint Verification Aura:** Verified NFC gates and completed rings cast a localized glow using `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses a balanced rounded geometry (Level 2) that feels ergonomic and high-tech, avoiding overly sharp industrial edges while maintaining a modern, athletic profile.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Standard Cards & Modules (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) corner radius for fitness summary cards, calendar modules, and workout split blocks.
- **Glass Modals & Digital Membership Pass (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px) corner radius for high-visibility membership passes and bottom sheets.
- **Buttons, Badges & Interactive Chips:** Pill radius (`9999px`) for full tactile fluidity during active sessions, preventing tap target confusion.
- **Small Controls & Metric Tickers:** `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius for input fields, micro telemetry cells, and set counter boxes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Action:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), solid `#FF5E1E` background, `#FFFFFF` text (`label-lg`), subtle top-edge highlight (`inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`), and an ambient orange glow on focus/tap.
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** Pill-shaped, translucent surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, perimeter border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, text `#F9FAFB`. Hover/active shifts background to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Success / Check-In Action:** Pill-shaped, solid `#10B981`, `#042F2E` high-contrast bold typography for verified QR entry points.

### Digital Membership Pass
- **Card Chassis:** Multi-layered frosted card (`rounded-xl`, `backdrop-blur: 20px`, surface `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.75)` with top perimeter border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`).
- **Security Visualizer:** Holographic orange-to-ember gradient border track and integrated live QR/NFC dynamic waveform code.
- **Status Indicator:** Glowing pill badge (`#10B981`) anchored top-right with label "CLUB ACCESS: ACTIVE".

### Telemetry Cards & Metric Tickers
- **Container:** Dark carbon glass card with `padding: 1rem` (`space-md`), micro-border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Layout:** Top row holds category label in `label-sm` (`#9CA3AF`) with a matching fitness vector icon; main row showcases `telemetry-num` stat in `#F9FAFB` flanked by micro-unit label (e.g., `BPM`, `KCAL`, `KG`); bottom row displays a dynamic sparkline or mint status increment badge (`+12%`).

### Interactive Chips & Workout Filters
- **Default:** Translucent surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, text `#9CA3AF`, pill shape.
- **Selected:** Border `#FF5E1E`, background `rgba(255, 94, 30, 0.15)`, text `#FF5E1E`, accompanied by a glowing indicator dot.

### Lists & Exercise Row Items
- **Structure:** Separated item blocks with `margin-bottom: 0.5rem` (`space-sm`) rather than plain divider lines.
- **Surface:** Subtle obsidian card (`#111827`), hover/press state introduces `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 94, 30, 0.4)`.
- **Visuals:** Leading thumbnail shows equipment or muscle icon; center stack holds exercise name and set breakdown; trailing area contains quick-tap check-off circles with an animated mint fill.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Chassis:** `0.5rem` radius, background `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#FF5E1E` with an outer ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(255, 94, 30, 0.2)`.
- **Placeholder Text:** `body-md` in `#4B5563`. Text input in `#F9FAFB`.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Controls
- **Radio & Checkbox:** `0.25rem` or circular tokens with border `2px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. Checked state transitions immediately to `#10B981` or `#FF5E1E` with an inner white icon mark.
- **Interactive Toggles:** Pill track with dark recessed well (`#0B0F17`), switching to solid energetic orange (`#FF5E1E`) when engaged, with a tactile white knob casting an ambient shadow.
